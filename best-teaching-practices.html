Summary

4 Golden Rules for Every Educator

Kal se hi apni classroom teaching mein yeh best practices implement kijiye:

1. Prompt with Context

Topic + Grade + 5-min Real World Hook + Fast-learner challenge. 10 second mein structured flow.

2. Visual Delivery

Text definitions ratane ke badle screen par 3D interactive model run karein — retention 5x badhta hai.

3. Gamified & Diagnostic

Bubble shooter games + 10-Q diagnostic assessments se bache ka exact learning gap identify karein.

4. Focus on Mentorship

Routine paperwork AI sambhalega, teacher ka primary focus bachho ki confidence aur care par hoga.
